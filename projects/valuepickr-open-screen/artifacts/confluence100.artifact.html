The philosophy: strong business + reasonable valuation + rising 30W trend + real momentum — a good company at a fair price that the market is already starting to reward, not just quietly trending up in isolation. How the ranking and confidence % are built

Universe. 616 stocks have been deepdived by the ValuePickr open-screen pipeline. Both lists hard-drop any name with a red_flag_tier (HIGH CAUTION / AVOID / EXCLUDE — dropped, not sized down). Overall then ranks all 438 remaining names — a 10x thesis is not required, so a high-quality business that only clears the return bar at 3-4x still appears (tagged non-thesis). Thesis only keeps the 164 where thesis_fit is 10x-in-2-3-years or 100x-in-10-years. Thesis-only is a strict subset of Overall. Both are a different, larger universe than the live Rs 1L portfolio — only a handful of names overlap (marked HOLD).

Master score is this pipeline's own composite — 0.25×conviction + 0.25×quality + 0.20×expectation-gap + 0.15×consistency + 0.15×asymmetry, renormalized over whichever components exist for that stock (see MASTER_SCORE_METHODOLOGY.md). Coverage is thin almost everywhere: only 12 names have 4-5 of those components computed — the rest are still mostly riding on conviction_score alone, with the deepdive's own qualitative four_box read (tailwind / TAM / moat / valuation, scored 0-5) blended in to partly compensate, weighted more heavily the thinner the coverage.

Trend quality — 18% of confidence. For each of the top candidates a live weekly chart was pulled (Yahoo Finance, 3-year history) and a 30-week EMA computed — the same discipline the live portfolio runs on. This produces a 0-100 technical_score: a fresh cross (within 3 weeks) or a tight cushion above the line (≤15%) scores highest; sitting below the EMA scores near the floor (a stock can be fundamentally excellent and still fail the entry-timing filter); very extended (>45% above) is discounted too, since that's poor risk-reward to buy into, not a red flag on the business. Names that couldn't be ticker-matched carry a neutral-low score rather than a real technical read. The Top 10 list additionally hard-requires being above the EMA — see below.

Momentum — 12% of confidence. A separate 0-100 momentum_score, new as of 2026-09-22: trailing 13-week and 26-week absolute price return (the same two windows the swing-trading screen uses), blended with the stock's relative strength vs the Nifty 500 over the same 13-week window. This is orthogonal to trend quality — a stock can be a fresh EMA cross with only modest momentum, or a strong-momentum name that's simply always traded above its line. Genuinely outperforming the index or posting strong absolute returns scores highest; quietly lagging the broad market or drifting down scores lowest, even if the chart still looks fine in isolation.

Confidence % is a model output, not a probability of return. Treat ≥70 as "worth a full-position look," 50-69 as "real but thin, verify before sizing," and <50 as "on the radar, not yet actionable." No stock here was sourced by open-ended web search — every name already existed in the ValuePickr deepdive database. Rebuilt every vpscreen-rerank cycle (no weekly gate) so a tier move or score shift shows up the same day.
