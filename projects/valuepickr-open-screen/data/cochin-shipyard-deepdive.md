# Deepdive: Cochin Shipyard Limited (COCHINSHIP)
**Initiated:** 2026-10-03  
**Signal source:** Global-proxy-scan 2026-09-29 (HD Hyundai accelerating, 3.5-yr backlog; Sep 7 DAC ₹1.1T procurement)  
**Thesis:** Capacity-constrained India shipbuilder positioned for secular tailwind (defense modernization + commercial vessel shortage)

---

## Investment Thesis

### Global Context
- **HD Hyundai Heavy Industries Q2 2026:** Revenue +19% YoY, operating profit +74% YoY, backlog >500 vessels = 3.5-year lead time
- **Implication:** Global shipyard capacity sold out through 2030; India yards will see order pull-through as customers avoid Korean/Chinese wait-lists
- **Trigger:** Sep 7, 2026 Defence Acquisition Council approved ₹1.10 lakh crore ($11.6B) procurement with 98% domestic content — includes marine platforms & propulsion

### Cochin Shipyard Specifics
1. **Capacity Expansion (2024–2026):** New 310m × 75m dry dock (amongst India's largest) enables construction of:
   - LNG carriers (typical 300m length)
   - VLCC tankers (typical 330m length)
   - Large naval platforms (destroyers, frigates)
   - Offshore support vessels
   
2. **Historical Role:** Primarily defense (Indian Navy indigenous platforms) + niche commercial (tankers, bulkers <200m)

3. **DAC Tailwind:** The ₹1.1T procurement includes:
   - Naval vessel platforms (not just propulsion/missiles)
   - Submarine-related platforms
   - Large commercial vessels (government strategic shipbuilding vision targets 5% global share by 2030, up from <1% now)

### Key Financials to Check
- **Q1 FY27 order inflows:** Target >₹1,500 cr (vs historical avg ₹800–1,200 cr)
- **Capacity utilization:** New dock should begin contributing FY27-FY28
- **Order book:** Target >₹8,000 cr (3-4x FY revenue for multi-year visibility)
- **Margins:** Defense contracts typically 12–15% EBITDA; commercial (tankers) 8–10%
