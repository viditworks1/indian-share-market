## Investment Thesis

- **Implication:** Defense radar/EW/sensor cycle is multi-year structural, not cyclical — long-cycle procurement locking in capacity for 2026–2030

1. **Existing Order Book (as of Q1 FY27):**
   - Uttam Radar (indigenous naval surveillance radar): Astra won ₹2,205 cr order in FY26/FY27 — largest-ever order for the company
   - Defense ministry radar/EW programs: Astra is a Tier-1 radar supplier (alongside BEL)
   - Production ramp: Uttam radar production expected to scale FY27–FY29

2. **Sep 7 DAC Tailwind:**
   - Arudhra Radars for Navy (manufactured by BEL, but Astra may have subcontract/supply role)
   - Broader radar modernization pipeline (Arudhra + replacement of aging systems)
   - Defense electronics sector allocated ~₹25,000 cr+ from ₹1.1T DAC procurement

3. **Competitive Position:**
   - Primary competitor: Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL) — but BEL is upstream (design/manufacturing)
   - Astra is specialized supplier (EW, comms, subsystems)
   - Market: India defense radar/EW/comms market is ~₹5,000–7,000 cr annually; Astra captures ~₹200–400 cr (4–8%)
