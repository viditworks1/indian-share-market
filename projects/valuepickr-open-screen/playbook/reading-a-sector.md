# Reading a Sector: Breadth vs Depth

**Analyst breadth ≠ Investor breadth** — phreakv6, #345 (2026-09-12, topic 180929):  
Analysts must have breadth across sub-sectors (APIs, CDMO, hospitals, diagnostics). Individual investors can afford to be "inch-wide in breadth, mile-deep in depth"—focus on the most interesting sub-sector only, with no external job constraints.

Real edge comes from **latticework of mental models** (about the world, people, history, psychology)—not from studying businesses alone. Analysts weight all information equally (risk management); investors must discern the 2-3 things that matter and ignore the noise (signal-to-noise ratio is the game).
