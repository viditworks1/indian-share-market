Every Monday a new Rs 1,00,000 paper portfolio is decided from the live recommendation, priced at the prior Friday's close, and then never touched again. Two sizing philosophies run in parallel — standard (diversified) and concentrated (top-5 by a two-factor composite). A separate live recommendation tracker stays continuously rebalanced to whatever the recommendation doc says right now, for contrast against the frozen cohorts. A weekly, frozen 6-month swing cohort series (momentum + dated catalysts, hard stops) runs alongside for a third angle. Marked to market every weekday; nothing here is advice.
