## Summary: Why W36 Logic Works for These 8 Names

The 6-month swing portfolio succeeds because:

1. **Momentum + Catalyst = Power Play** — Names with both +30% momentum AND a catalyst within 6 months are historically the best short-term performers
2. **Technical Confirmation** — Rising 30W/10W EMAs + nearness to 52W high means this is *real* momentum, not stale
3. **No Vertical Chases** — The +40% extension filter means we catch the move *early* in its arc, not after a 100% run already (lower crash risk)
4. **Quality Floor** — Screening out red-flag tiers and cash-burners avoids the "momentum trap" (the name crashes on guidance miss)
5. **Dated Catalysts** — "Q2 results ~Nov" is more actionable than "eventual inflection" for a 6-month hold
